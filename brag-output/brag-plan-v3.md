# Brag Plan v3: Offscript — "DER DAUMEN"

## Source note (unchanged from v2, and still true)
offscript.ch remains unreachable from this environment — `curl` and `WebFetch`
are both refused by the egress proxy (`CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403`,
`kind: connect_rejected`), as is `www.offscript.ch`. Nothing here is quoted
from the live site.

**What is factual and comes from the repo:** the four service names (CONTENT,
STRATEGIE, SOCIAL, CREATOR), the production stages, the palette, the logo and
the URL. **What is newly written for this cut:** every line of display copy,
including the closing line `WIR STOPPEN DEN DAUMEN.` — that is a proposed
tagline, not something Offscript already says, and it needs their sign-off
before this goes anywhere public. No statistic, client name, capability claim
or service description has been invented; the reach figure used in v1/v2 is
deliberately absent here.

## Brief for this cut
Three things were asked for: phones and cameras on screen, more motion
elements, and motion that reads as smooth — with new copy and a real hook.

## The angle
v1 led with the brand's own hook line. v2 took its idea from the agency's name.
v3 takes it from the only thing that actually decides whether content works:
**the thumb.**

The film opens inside the problem rather than describing it — a phone, a feed
already moving, content going past faster than anyone could read it. The hook
lands over that motion at 1.07s: **NIEMAND WARTET AUF DEINEN POST.** The scroll
keeps accelerating for another three seconds, blurring as it goes, and then
decelerates into a full stop on one red card. That card is not a cut — it grows
out of its own place in the phone and becomes the whole frame, and the promise
is delivered on it: **ALSO GIB IHNEN EINEN GRUND.**

Everything after that is what "a reason" costs: a camera, a process, and four
phones with something on them worth stopping for.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Motion first, words second. The feed is already running at frame one; the phone
settles; the hook lands on the first beat and the scroll gets *faster* under it,
which is the opposite of what a title card usually does. The kicker
**DER DAUMEN ENTSCHEIDET.** arrives in red at 3.18s, right before the stop.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The scroll itself.** Eighteen feed cards on a strip that accelerates to
  800 px/s, holds flat, and decelerates to rest — three chained tweens whose
  velocities match at the joins, with motion blur that rises and resolves on
  the same curve, and a scroll-rail thumb tracking all three phases.
- **The stop → the takeover.** The feed lands on the red card and the card
  becomes the frame, its corner radius unwinding as it grows. Three more cards
  keep going and leave through the top of the frame.
- **The camera chapter.** The next chapter scrolls up into the frame and
  pushes the red out ahead of it at a third of its speed — a parallax handoff,
  not a cut. A viewfinder closes onto the corners, a camera draws in and keeps
  drifting, its iris opens, REC pulses, one light pass crosses the dark plate,
  and the four production stages stack up on the beat:
  **PLANEN · DREHEN · SCHNEIDEN · POSTEN.**
- **Four phones.** The dark chapter scrolls away and four devices rise into a
  row on the beat grid, each breathing at its own rate and phase, each screen
  scrolling its own content at its own speed, with the services named beneath
  them on one shared baseline.

## Outro / punchline
Paper, stillness, the logo, **offscript.ch**, one red rule, and the line that
closes the hook: **WIR STOPPEN DEN DAUMEN.** The last second is completely
static and silent.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`, with the motion budget spent on continuity rather than
  on effects
- One loud moment (the stop), one dark chapter, stillness at both ends

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080 · Duration: 21.6s

## Visual identity
Paper `#F5F3EF` · Ink `#111111` · Muted `#6E6A63` · Offscript red `#F20505`
(lifted to `#FF4433` on navy) · Navy `#0E1626`. Display: system sans 800, tight
negative tracking. Meta: system mono, letterspaced.

## Audio direction
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9` (114.84 BPM) — the third different
  track across the three cuts. Chosen because its beat grid is already strong
  at 1.07s, which is where this hook has to land; a track with a quiet intro
  would have fought the opening.
- Beats used: 1.07 / 1.59 (hook) · 3.18 (kicker) · 5.28 (the stop) ·
  6.34 / 6.86 (the promise) · 8.96 (chapter push) · 10.01 / 10.54 / 11.06 /
  11.60 (production stages) · 12.65 (chapter push) · 13.70 / 14.22 / 14.76 /
  15.28 (the four phones) · 17.91 / 18.44 / 19.48 (endcard).
- SFX: one continuous air bed under the accelerating feed (texture, not an
  event), one riser that peaks exactly on the stop, two layers on the stop
  itself — the only stacked moment in the film — two swipes for the two chapter
  pushes, four tiny marks under the production stages, and one release under
  the address. Nothing on the hook lines, nothing on the phones, nothing after
  19.5s.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Der Feed — 5.28s (0.00 → 5.28)
A phone settles on paper over a slowly drifting dot field; the feed inside is
already moving and keeps speeding up, blurring as it goes. UI chips rise past
it on a stagger. **NIEMAND WARTET** (1.07) / **AUF DEINEN POST.** (1.59) land
alongside; a red rule draws at 3.18 under **DER DAUMEN ENTSCHEIDET.** From 4.30
the feed decelerates and comes to rest on a red card.
Reading budget: 5 words, up for 4s. Met with room.
Audio: air bed, then a riser from 3.00 peaking on the stop.

### Scene 2 — Der Stopp — 3.68s (5.28 → 8.96)
The red card holds for four frames, then grows out of the phone into the whole
frame, its radius unwinding. Three cards carry on past the stop and leave the
top of the frame. **DER DAUMEN BLEIBT STEHEN.** (6.10, mono) then
**ALSO GIB IHNEN** (6.34) / **EINEN GRUND.** (6.86), white on red.
Reading budget: 4 words held for 2.1s settled. Met.
Audio: impact plus sub on the stop. Nothing on the lines.

### Scene 3 — Produktion — 3.69s (8.96 → 12.65)
The dark chapter scrolls up into frame and pushes the red out ahead of it. A
viewfinder closes onto the corners, REC comes up, the camera draws in and its
iris opens, one light pass crosses. **01 PLANEN** (10.01) · **02 DREHEN**
(10.54) · **03 SCHNEIDEN** (11.06) · **04 POSTEN** (11.60) stack up and hold
together to 12.65.
Reading budget: the stages accumulate, so the first is readable for 2.6s and
the last for 1.05s. Met.
Audio: swipe on the push, four small marks on the stages.

### Scene 4 — Was wir machen — 4.73s (12.65 → 17.38)
The dark plate scrolls away; four phones rise into a row on 13.70 / 14.22 /
14.76 / 15.28, each with its index and service name: **01 CONTENT · 02
STRATEGIE · 03 SOCIAL · 04 CREATOR**. Every device breathes at its own rate and
every screen scrolls its own content, so no two are ever in lockstep. All four
hold together to 16.86, then the row leaves downward.
Reading budget: the names accumulate; the first is up for 3.2s.
Audio: swipe on the push. Nothing on the phones.

### Scene 5 — Die Adresse — 4.22s (17.38 → 21.60)
Logo (17.91), **offscript.ch** (18.44), one red rule drawn beneath it (19.48),
and **WIR STOPPEN DEN DAUMEN.** (20.02). Static and silent from 20.45.

## Scene duration check
5.28 + 3.68 + 3.69 + 4.73 + 4.22 = **21.60s** — inside the 15-25s law, in the
18-22s sweet spot.
