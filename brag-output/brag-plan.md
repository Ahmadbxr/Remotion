# Brag Plan: Offscript

## What is this app?
Offscript is a Zürich content agency that makes short-form social content for
brands — content built specifically not to get scrolled past. It is a service
business, not an app: there is no UI, no signup, no dashboard. The product is
the content itself, and the brand's own material is the only honest source.

## The angle
The agency's own hook is an insult wrapped in a compliment:

> DEINE MARKE IST NICHT LANGWEILIG. DEIN CONTENT VIELLEICHT SCHON.
> (Your brand isn't boring. Your content might be.)

That line does the whole job — it flatters the prospect and indicts their feed
in the same breath. The video's premise is simply to let that land, then show
the agency is equipped to fix it. No invented claims, no SaaS language: every
word on screen is copy Offscript already uses.

What makes it specific: the brand's visual language is warm paper and exactly
one violent red, and the red is never decoration — it arrives as a takeover.
The video is built around that single gesture.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Warm paper. Oversized Helvetica, left-aligned, hard against the type's own
weight: **DEINE MARKE IST NICHT LANGWEILIG.** Held long enough to read as a
compliment — then **DEIN CONTENT VIELLEICHT SCHON.** lands underneath in red
on the 1.60s beat and turns it into an accusation. The hook is the turn, not
the typography.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The red takeover.** The red does not fade in; it floods out of the word
  that earned it and takes the whole frame. The agency's promise is delivered
  white-on-red: *WIR MACHEN CONTENT, DEN MAN NICHT WEGSWIPT.*
- **The service system, arriving one by one.** 01/04 CONTENT, 02/04 STRATEGIE,
  03/04 SOCIAL, 04/04 CREATOR — indexed against a persistent red rail. This is
  the closest thing the agency has to a "product screen", and it is real: it is
  the service list, in the brand's own indexing.
- **The process as one horizontal chain.** IDEA → SHOOT → EDIT → POST → GROW,
  connected by the same red line, resolving on the outcome: **100K+**. In
  landscape this reads as a workflow, which is exactly the credential an
  agency is selling.

## Outro / punchline
Back to paper, all noise gone. The logo, then **offscript.ch** set large with
a red rule drawn under it, and the tagline *CONTENT, DER HÄNGEN BLEIBT.*
(content that sticks). The punchline is the restraint: after a red takeover
and a five-stage chain, the agency stops shouting and just leaves its address.

## User flow worth showing
none — service business, no app. The centerpiece leans on the strongest real
material instead: the service system (Q3/Q4) and the process chain, both of
which are genuine agency artefacts rather than landing-page decoration.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: a Zürich agency that does not need to shout
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, confidence through restraint.
  Type does the work; motion is decisive but never busy. The one permitted
  loud moment is the red takeover, and it is loud because everything around
  it is quiet. No chaotic cutting, no flashing text, no zoom spam.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20.5s

Landscape is deliberate: Offscript already has a 9:16 reel. This is the
landscape counterpart — a pitch you put on a site or send to a client, not
another social post. The process chain is landscape-native and reads far
better wide than tall.

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#F5F3EF` (warm off-white / paper)
- Accent: `#F20505` (Offscript red — strategic, never flooding a frame except
  deliberately)
- Text: `#111111` (near-black)
- Secondary text: `#6E6A63` (warm grey)
- Dark surface: `#0E1626` (very dark navy — not black; black flattens the red)
- On navy, red lifts to `#FF4433` — `#F20505` sits near 3:1 on navy and is
  marginal as text
- Display font: Helvetica Neue (system sans stack), weight 800, tight negative
  tracking
- Body/meta font: system monospace (`ui-monospace`, SF Mono), letterspaced, for
  index numbers and labels
- Strongest visual element: the red as a *takeover*, and the red rail that
  indexes the services and then becomes the process line
- Logo: `public/offscript-logo.png` — black brush wordmark with a red "zh".
  Black on transparent, so it must sit on paper, never on navy.

## Share copy (draft)
Your brand isn't boring. Your content might be. — Offscript, Zürich.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse, motion-matched accents. The music carries the
  pace; SFX only mark the four moments that actually change state.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM).
  Chosen over the 120 BPM track because polished wants room, and over the
  110 BPM tracks because its early strong cues fall where this story needs
  them.
- Music treatment: start at 0.00, in under the hook, sit low (bed, not lead).
  Lift slightly into the red takeover, hold through the middle, and fade out
  across the final card so the URL is read in near-silence.
- Music cue guidance: preset read from
  `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-...music-cues.md`.
  Target strong cues: **1.60s** (the turn — second hook line), **3.70s** (the
  red takeover), **12.65s** (the process chain begins), **17.91s** (the logo
  settles). Beat grid for the sequential service reveal: **8.44 / 9.50 /
  10.54 / 11.60** — every other beat, ~1.06s apart, comfortably above the
  0.8s readability floor for short labels.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none. This brand is restrained; reactive glow or
  breathing would betray it.
- SFX posture: sparse. Four cues at most: the red takeover, the service
  arrivals, the chain resolving on 100K+, and the rule drawing under the URL.
- Audio-coupled moments: the four service labels arriving one by one; the red
  flood; the 100K+ landing; the red rule drawing left-to-right under
  offscript.ch.
- Restraint rule: no whoosh on every transition, no riser before every scene,
  no sound on the tagline. Silence under the final URL is the point — if the
  last 1.5 seconds are busy, the address does not land.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The turn — 3.7s (0.00 → 3.70)
Warm paper, empty. Oversized display type arrives left-aligned:
**DEINE MARKE IST NICHT LANGWEILIG.** — set large, settling by ~1.0s and held
so it reads as a compliment. On the 1.60s strong cue, **DEIN CONTENT
VIELLEICHT SCHON.** lands beneath it in red. Both lines then hold together to
~3.6s: the joke only works when both are on screen at once.
Reading budget: line 1 is 5 words (~1.5s settled), line 2 is 4 words (~1.2s
settled). Both met.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two-stage line reveal; the second line is the
payload and must land on the beat, not before it.
Audio intent: music enters low and unhurried; nothing competes with the read.
Audio-coupled idea: none — let the line land dry. A sound here would make it
feel like a joke being announced.
Music: warm, low, entering.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2 (the red does not crossfade, it takes over)

### Scene 2 — The red takeover — 3.68s (3.70 → 7.38)
On the 3.70s strong cue the red floods the frame — expanding out of the second
line rather than fading in over it. White on `#F20505`:
**WIR MACHEN CONTENT, DEN MAN NICHT WEGSWIPT.** Set large, one or two lines,
held to ~7.1s.
Reading budget: 7 words → ~2.1s settled minimum. Met with room.
Sequential/interaction: none — this is a single declarative statement and
should arrive whole.
Audio intent: the one loud moment. Weight and commitment, not a whoosh.
Audio-coupled idea: the flood itself — a single impact with real low end,
landing exactly on the takeover, not before it.
Music: lifts slightly under the red.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3 (red recedes to paper; the rail survives)

### Scene 3 — The service system — 5.27s (7.38 → 12.65)
Back to paper. A vertical red rail draws at the left. Four services arrive
against it, one per every-other-beat, each with its mono index:
**01/04 CONTENT** (8.44) · **02/04 STRATEGIE** (9.50) · **03/04 SOCIAL**
(10.54) · **04/04 CREATOR** (11.60). Each label holds ~1.06s settled; all four
remain on screen together from 11.60 to 12.65 so the set reads as a system
rather than a list that scrolled past.
Reading budget: short labels, 0.8s floor — 1.06s spacing clears it.
Sequential/interaction: yes — four labels arriving one by one on the beat
grid, indexed, against a rail that persists across all four.
Audio intent: tactile and quiet. Four small, related accents — a family, not
four copies of one sound.
Audio-coupled idea: one micro accent per service arrival, varied slightly so
the fourth is not audibly the first again.
Music: steady bed, unchanged.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4 (the rail rotates from vertical to horizontal)

### Scene 4 — Process to proof — 4.21s (12.65 → 16.86)
On the 12.65s strong cue the rail lays down into a horizontal line and the
process reads across it: **IDEA → SHOOT → EDIT → POST → GROW**, the stages
connecting rather than appearing independently. The frame goes dark navy
(`#0E1626`) as the chain completes, and it resolves on the outcome: **100K+**,
set large, with **GROW** above it in lifted red (`#FF4433`). Held to ~16.7s.
Reading budget: five one-word stages revealed across ~2.2s, then the number
held ~1.6s alone. The number is the thing to remember, so it gets the hold.
Sequential/interaction: yes — five stages appearing along one connecting line,
left to right, then a single number landing.
Audio intent: momentum building to one resolution. The stages should feel
connected, the number should feel arrived-at.
Audio-coupled idea: light ticks along the chain; one weighted landing on
100K+ — the second and last time low end is allowed.
Music: holds, then thins going into the close.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5 (navy lifts back to paper)

### Scene 5 — The address — 3.64s (16.86 → 20.50)
Paper again, and quiet. The Offscript logo settles on the 17.91s strong cue.
Beneath it, **offscript.ch** set large — this is the one element the whole
video exists to deliver — with a thin red rule drawing left-to-right under it,
once. Then the tagline in warm grey: **CONTENT, DER HÄNGEN BLEIBT.** Everything
is static for the final ~1.3s.
Reading budget: the URL gets ~2.5s fully settled; the tagline 4 words (~1.2s).
Sequential/interaction: yes — logo, then URL, then the rule drawing under it,
then the tagline. Strictly in that order; the rule is the only motion after
the URL is up.
Audio intent: release. Music fades out under the URL.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft accent as the red rule draws. Nothing on the
tagline, nothing after.
Music: fading to silence by ~19.5s.
Transition mood: — (end)

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat-but-restrained corporate bed, used as a
floor rather than a driver.
**Audio summary:** Music enters low under a dry hook, lifts once for the red
takeover, holds a steady bed through the services and the chain, marks exactly
four state changes with sparse motion-matched accents, and fades out under the
final card so offscript.ch is read in near-silence.

## Scene duration check
3.70 + 3.68 + 5.27 + 4.21 + 3.64 = **20.50s** — inside the 15-25s law, in the
18-22s sweet spot.
