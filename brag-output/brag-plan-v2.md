# Brag Plan v2: Offscript — "DAS SKRIPT"

## Source note (read this first)
The brief was to pull everything from the live site. **offscript.ch is not
reachable from this environment** — both `curl` and `WebFetch` are refused by
the egress proxy (`CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403`,
`kind: connect_rejected`), and `www.offscript.ch` is refused identically. No
page content was retrieved, so nothing here is quoted from the live site.

Everything on screen is therefore drawn from Offscript brand material already
in this repository (`src/reel/`, `src/theme.ts`, `public/offscript-logo.png`):
the hook, the four service names, the five process stages, the reach figure,
the tagline, the palette and the logo. **No claim, number, client name or
service description has been invented.** The deliberately generic agency
boilerplate in Scene 1 is written as a parody of industry language — it is
explicitly *not* Offscript copy, and the video's whole point is that it gets
crossed out.

## What is this?
Offscript is a Zürich content agency making short-form social content for
brands. It is a service business — no app, no UI, no signup. The product is
the content, so the brand's own material is the only honest source.

## The angle — a different one from v1
v1 led with the hook and treated the red as a takeover. v2 takes its idea from
the agency's **name** instead, which is the strongest asset they have and which
v1 never touched.

Everyone in this industry reads from the same script. Offscript is the one that
doesn't. So the video opens *on that script* — four lines of the beige,
interchangeable agency language every brand has heard — lets it sit just long
enough to be recognised, and then crosses the whole thing out with one red
stroke. The header line above the script turns over from
**DAS SKRIPT, DAS ALLE LESEN.** to **OFF SCRIPT.** in the same position, at the
same size: the brand name *is* the punchline of the gesture.

Everything after that is what happens once the script is gone.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Paper, a document header, a hairline, and generic agency copy printing itself
in line by line with a soft key tick under each. It reads as boredom on
purpose — which is the one thing this audience will recognise instantly. The
hook is the recognition, and the payoff is 3.5s later when it gets struck.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The strike (3.52s).** One red diagonal, drawn bottom-left to top-right
  across all four lines in 0.22s. The script dims. This is the video's thesis
  in one gesture.
- **The red flood (4.02s).** The red does not fade in — it expands out of the
  slash's own centre and takes the frame. White on red:
  *DEINE MARKE IST NICHT LANGWEILIG.* Then, in ink rather than white so the
  turn gets a colour turn too: *DEIN CONTENT VIELLEICHT SCHON.*
- **The services build the frame (8.02 → 12.02s).** Four navy columns wipe up
  from the bottom, one per second: 01 CONTENT · 02 STRATEGIE · 03 SOCIAL ·
  04 CREATOR. The background transition is not applied to the services — it is
  *made of* them. By the fourth column the frame is a dark chapter.
- **The measuring rule becomes the process spine (12.02s).** A single red rule
  grows a quarter at a time as each column arrives — it measures the four
  services. When they lift away it drops into the middle of the frame and is
  the process line: IDEA → SHOOT → EDIT → POST → GROW, revealed by a playhead
  running along it. One DOM element, visibly the same object in both roles.
- **The number (16.02s).** The counter rolls 3K → 12K → 47K → **100K+**,
  landing on the track's single biggest bass cue.

## Outro / punchline
Paper returns, everything stops. Logo, **offscript.ch**, one red rule drawn
under it, and the tagline *CONTENT, DER HÄNGEN BLEIBT.* The last 1.5s are
completely static and near-silent. After a struck-out script and a red flood,
the restraint is the flex.

## User flow worth showing
None — service business, no app. The centrepiece is the service system and the
process chain, both real agency artefacts.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`, pushed harder than v1
- Creative direction: a Zürich agency that does not need to shout — but that
  is willing to cross something out in front of you
- One loud moment (the strike + flood), one big moment (the number), and
  stillness at both ends

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080 · Duration: 21.6s
Landscape again: Offscript already has a 9:16 reel; this is the site/pitch cut.
The four-column service system and the horizontal process spine are both
landscape-native.

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Paper `#F5F3EF` · Ink `#111111` · Muted `#6E6A63`
- Offscript red `#F20505`; on navy it lifts to `#FF4433`
- Dark surface `#0E1626` (navy, not black — black flattens the red)
- Display: Helvetica Neue / system sans, 800, tight negative tracking
- Meta: system mono, letterspaced, for indices and document chrome
- Logo: `assets/img/logo.png`, black wordmark with a red "zh" — paper only,
  never on navy

## Audio direction
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1` (120.19 BPM) — chosen over v1's
  114.84 BPM track because this cut needs a **quiet intro** for the script to
  print into (the track's beat grid only starts at 3.02s) and a genuinely big
  section for the number. Its intensity peaks from 16.02s, and the counter
  lands exactly there.
- Beat grid used: 3.52 (strike) · 4.02 (flood) · 4.52 / 6.03 (the two hook
  lines) · 8.02 / 9.02 / 10.02 / 11.02 (the four columns, every other beat,
  1.0s apart — clear of the 0.8s readability floor) · 12.02 (the rule drops) ·
  16.02 (100K+, intensity 0.9988, bass 1.0) · 17.52 / 18.02 / 19.02 (endcard).
- Music treatment: in low under the printing script, up on the flood, up again
  into the dark chapter, and faded to silence across the endcard so the address
  is read in near-silence.
- SFX posture: sparse, and grouped into families rather than one sound per
  event — four related key ticks for the four printed lines, two alternating
  panel switches for the four columns, and exactly three singular sounds in the
  whole video (the strike, the flood, the landing).
- Audio-reactive treatment: none. This brand is restrained.
- Restraint rule: nothing on the hook lines (the line lands dry — a sound there
  would announce the joke), nothing on the tagline, nothing after 19.02s.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Das Skript — 4.02s (0.00 → 4.02, held to 4.60 under the flood)
Paper. Header **DAS SKRIPT, DAS ALLE LESEN.** in mono over a hairline that
draws left to right. Four lines of generic agency language print in, each
revealed by a paper mask retracting in ten visible steps — printing, not
fading — at 0.50 / 1.20 / 1.90 / 2.55. All four hold together to 3.52.
On 3.52 one red diagonal is drawn across the whole block in 0.22s and the
lines dim to 45%.
Reading budget: four lines, ~0.65s apart, all four up for a full second before
the strike. They are meant to be skimmed, not studied — that is the joke.
Sequential: yes — four printed lines, then one strike across all of them.
Audio: music bed only, low. One soft key tick per line, four different keys.
Audio-coupled: the strike — a short rip, exactly on the stroke.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Off script — 4.00s (4.02 → 8.02)
On 4.02 the red expands out of the slash's own centre and floods the frame.
The header turns over in place: **OFF SCRIPT.** white on red. Then, at 4.52,
**DEINE MARKE IST NICHT LANGWEILIG.** in white; at 6.03,
**DEIN CONTENT VIELLEICHT SCHON.** in ink. Both hold together to 7.50.
The red then retracts upward off-frame, revealing paper from the bottom.
Reading budget: 5 words then 4 words, ~1.5s and ~1.5s settled. Met.
Sequential: yes — two-stage line reveal; the second line is the payload.
Audio: one weighted low impact on the flood. Nothing on either line.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3 (red lifts, paper is revealed upward)

### Scene 3 — Was ohne Skript entsteht — 4.00s (8.02 → 12.02)
Four navy columns wipe up from the bottom on 8.02 / 9.02 / 10.02 / 11.02,
each carrying a mono index in lifted red and its service name in white:
**01 CONTENT · 02 STRATEGIE · 03 SOCIAL · 04 CREATOR**. A red rule above them
grows one quarter per column — it is measuring the set. All four hold together
from 11.02 to 12.02 so the services read as a system, not a list.
Reading budget: short labels, 1.0s apart, clear of the 0.8s floor.
Sequential: yes — four columns on a one-second grid.
Audio: two alternating panel switches, quiet and mechanical. A family, not
four copies of one sound.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4 (the columns stay; only the labels leave)

### Scene 4 — Der Ablauf, und was dabei rauskommt — 5.08s (12.02 → 17.10)
The labels lift away and the measuring rule drops into the middle of the navy
frame: same element, new job. A playhead runs left to right along it and each
stage appears as it is passed — **IDEA → SHOOT → EDIT → POST → GROW**
(12.80 → 14.27). Under the line, **ERGEBNIS**, and a counter rolls
3K → 12K → 47K → **100K+**, the last step landing on 16.02, the track's
biggest cue. Held completely still for a full second after.
Reading budget: five one-word stages across 1.5s, then the number alone for
1.0s. The number is what should be remembered, so it gets the hold.
Sequential: yes — a playhead-driven reveal, then a four-step counter.
Audio: one warm landing on 100K+. Nothing on the stages — the playhead is the
rhythm and it does not need ticking.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5 (paper rises back over the navy)

### Scene 5 — Die Adresse — 4.50s (17.10 → 21.60)
Paper. The logo settles on 17.52, **offscript.ch** at 18.02, a red rule drawn
once beneath it on 19.02, and the tagline **CONTENT, DER HÄNGEN BLEIBT.** at
19.52. From 20.10 nothing moves and nothing sounds.
Reading budget: the URL is up for 3.5s, the tagline for 2.0s.
Sequential: yes — logo, URL, rule, tagline, strictly in that order.
Audio: one soft accent as the rule draws. Music already fading; silent by 20.2.
Transition mood: — (end)

**Music mood:** driving corporate bed with a quiet intro, used as a floor under
the script and as a real lift under the dark chapter.
**Audio summary:** four key ticks under a printing script, a rip and a low
impact on the strike and flood, two alternating switches for the four columns,
one warm landing on the number, one soft accent under the final rule, and
silence from 20.2s so the address is read in the quiet.

## Scene duration check
4.02 + 4.00 + 4.00 + 5.08 + 4.50 = **21.60s** — inside the 15-25s law, in the
18-22s sweet spot.
